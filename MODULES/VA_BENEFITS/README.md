# VA_BENEFITS — SQUAD BAT Division Module

**Version:** 0.1.0 (Framework)
**Status:** Active — routing engine built, tracks defined, ready for data expansion

---

## Purpose

Non-medical, non-housing VA programs. Every veteran has potential access to at least one of these tracks. Most don't know what they qualify for until someone maps it out for them.

---

## Qualification Gate

Discharge character evaluated first — same gate as Medical/Disability division.

| Discharge | Access |
|-----------|--------|
| Honorable | Full |
| General (Under Honorable) | Full (minor exceptions) |
| OTH | Limited — VR&E may still apply with SC disability; most education/loan/pension blocked |
| Dishonorable | Blocked — discharge upgrade required |

## Tracks

### 1. Education & Training
**GI Bill routing by era:**
- Post-9/11 → Chapter 33 (tuition + BAH + books, up to 36 months)
- Pre-9/11 → Chapter 30 Montgomery (check remaining entitlement)
- With SC rating (any %) → **VR&E Chapter 31** evaluated first — often more valuable than GI Bill; pays full costs without drawing down GI Bill entitlement
- High-paying tech field → VET TEC program
- Private/out-of-state school → Yellow Ribbon Program
- Dependents of 100% or deceased → Chapter 35 DEA, Fry Scholarship

**Key form:** VA Form 22-1990

### 2. Employment & Transition
- Currently separating → TAP + SkillBridge (up to 180 days civilian employer, still on military pay)
- Post-separation → American Job Centers (priority service), ask for LVER or DVOP specifically
- DOL VETS programs, VA Work Study (paid work at VA while on GI Bill)

**Note:** Most veterans don't know to ask for the veterans employment representative at Job Centers. The general staff have fewer resources.

### 3. Home Loans & Adaptive Housing
- VA Home Loan Guarantee: 0% down, no PMI, **reusable** (most vets don't know it can be used again)
- Certificate of Eligibility required (lenders can pull directly)
- SAH Grant: up to $109,986 for severely disabled vets needing adapted home
- SHA Grant: up to $22,036 for moderate adaptation needs

**Key form:** VA Form 26-1880 (COE)

### 4. Financial & Pension
- VA Pension: non-service-connected, wartime veterans, low income — unreimbursed medical expenses reduce countable income (many who appear ineligible actually qualify)
- VGLI: must enroll within 1 year of separation — **this window closes permanently**
- S-DVI: available within 2 years of new SC disability rating

**Key form:** VA Form 21P-527EZ (Pension)

### 5. Survivor & Dependent Benefits
- DIC: surviving spouse/children monthly payment if veteran died from SC condition or was 100% P&T for 10+ years
- CHAMPVA: healthcare coverage for dependents of 100% rated or deceased vets (75% of costs after deductible)
- Survivors Pension: low-income surviving spouses of wartime vets
- Fry Scholarship: children/spouses of members who died in line of duty

**Key form:** VA Form 21P-534EZ, VA Form 10-10d (CHAMPVA)

### 6. Burial & Memorial
- National Cemetery: veteran + eligible dependents, no cost
- Burial allowance: up to ~$796 + ~$796 plot (if SC death or died in VA care)
- Headstone/marker: government-furnished, any cemetery
- Pre-need eligibility: establish eligibility now so family doesn't have to during grief

**Key note:** Pre-need determination is one of the most compassionate things a veteran can do for their family. Almost no one does it.

---

## Critical Notes

**VR&E vs GI Bill:** Rated veterans should compare both before committing. VR&E (Ch.31) often pays more, covers more, and does not consume GI Bill entitlement. Rarely discussed by default.

**VGLI window:** One year from separation. No medical exam required if enrolled within 240 days. After one year, this benefit is gone.

**VA Pension:** Designed for wartime veterans with low income and no SC disability. Medical expenses dramatically reduce countable income — many vets who don't think they qualify, do.
