## Purpose

The federal government is legally required to award a percentage of contracts to Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Businesses (SDVOSB) and Veteran-Owned Small Businesses (VOSB).

The government regularly auctions surplus equipment, vehicles, and property — and veterans get priority access.

Most veterans who qualify for any of this have no idea it exists.

This division exists to cut through the red tape and get veterans to the door.

---

## Qualification Gate

| Discharge | Access |
|-----------|--------|
| Honorable / General | Full access to all certification and programs |
| OTH | VOSB/SDVOSB certification limited; SBA, SCORE, GSA Auctions, Bunker Labs generally open |
| Dishonorable | Certification blocked; pursue discharge upgrade; some resources still open |

## Tracks

### 1. SDVOSB / VOSB Certification

**Who qualifies:**
- Any veteran with honorable/general discharge who owns 51%+ and controls day-to-day operations → **VOSB**
- Same as above + service-connected disability (any rating) → **SDVOSB** (higher priority tier)

**Where to certify:**
- SBA VetCert (vetcert.sba.gov) — unified federal certification for both VOSB and SDVOSB
- Replaces old VA CVE process — one portal, same eligibility

**What you need:**
- DD-214
- Business formation documents (LLC articles, etc.)
- Proof of 51%+ ownership
- If SDVOSB: VA SC disability rating letter

**Colorado state level:**
- CDVBE (Colorado Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise) — separate from federal, required for CO state contracts
- Apply at colorado.gov/pacific/dpa/vendor-certification

**Why this matters:** Without certification you cannot access set-aside contracts, even if you are the most qualified bidder. Certification is the key.

### 2. Federal Contracting

**Required first steps:**
1. Register at SAM.gov — System for Award Management; required before any federal contract bid
2. Get your NAICS code(s) — industry classification codes used to find opportunities and set-asides
3. Build a Capability Statement — 1-page document: what you do, certifications, past performance, contact

**Where to find opportunities:**
- SAM.gov (sam.gov) — official federal contract opportunities
- GSA eBuy (ebuy.gsa.gov) — quotes for certified VOSBs
- USASpending.gov — research what agencies buy, who holds current contracts

**Free help:**
- Colorado PTAP (coloradoptap.com) — specialized procurement technical assistance; free; specifically for winning government contracts

---

### 3. SBA Programs

**Financing:**
- SBA 7(a) Loan — most flexible; Veterans Advantage reduces guaranty fees
- SBA 504 Loan — fixed-rate financing for major equipment or real estate
- SBA Microloan — up to $50k for startup costs

**Training:**
- Boots to Business — free 2-day entrepreneurship training; available to transitioning service members and families
- Register at: sbavets.force.com/s

**Mentorship (free):**
- SCORE (score.org/veteran) — retired executive mentors, chapter network nationwide
- SBDCs (coloradosbdc.org) — free business consulting, financial projections, business plans

---

### 4. GSA Surplus & Auctions

**What this is:** The federal government regularly disposes of surplus property — vehicles, IT equipment, electronics, furniture, construction equipment, lab equipment. Most of it is functional. Veterans and veteran-owned businesses get priority access.

**Where:**
- GSA Auctions: gsaauctions.gov — register for free, set up category alerts
- GSA Xcess: gsaxcess.gov — excess property before it goes to public auction (nonprofits and government entities first)

**How to get priority:**
- Have an active SAM.gov registration with VOSB/SDVOSB certification
- Note veteran status on GSA Auctions profile with DD-214

**What comes through:** Fleet vehicles, computers, servers, office furniture, scientific equipment, construction tools, aircraft parts, boats. Prices are often dramatically below market — this is one of the most underutilized veteran benefits in existence.

## The Three Steps Every Eligible Veteran Should Take

1. **Get certified at vetcert.sba.gov** — VOSB at minimum, SDVOSB if SC disabled
2. **Register at SAM.gov** — required for any federal contract or GSA priority access
3. **Register at gsaauctions.gov** — set up alerts for categories relevant to you

These three steps unlock a world most veterans never know exists. None of them cost money.
